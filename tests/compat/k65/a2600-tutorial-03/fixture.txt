The one real K65 program in the corpus, vendored from the original
distribution - see `PROVENANCE.md` for where it came from and why it is here
and not under `examples/`.

Its 196 lines reach almost everything sub-plan G closes: `bank`, a `var` comma
list, bare-call statement lines (`wsync hmove=a`), `align`, `image` with
`tiles`, `for ... eval` over `sin`, and evaluator brackets in operand position.
That is what makes it the task-by-task measure - the count below is expected to
fall at G2, G4 and G6 and to be zero except for `image` when G6 lands.

Today, nineteen diagnostics: five `K816-E0001` (the comma list and three
bare-call lines), nine `K816-E0101` (names the refused declarations would have
introduced), three `K816-K65-0001` (`image`) and two `K816-E0201` (evaluator
brackets).

`oracle.hex` is the original's own build: a raw 4 KiB cartridge image at
`$F000`, which is why this fixture needs `a2600.cfg` rather than the Atari XL
map the other oracle fixtures use.  K816 generates no entry stub and no bank
switching, so the row is compared at the instruction sequence and never at the
cartridge image.
