**`align` and `address` are accepted at the start of a code section, and
`align N + M` means "at an address congruent to M modulo N".**

The golden places `offset_table` at `$2004` (`align 16 + 4`), `aligned` at
`$2008` (`align 256 + 8`) and `addressed` at `$2040` (`address 0x2040`), and
zero-fills the gaps.

`Align` sits between `SecStart` and the body in `func`, `naked`, `main` *and*
`data` (`compiler.inc:121-136`), which answers the question probe 4 left as
"unverified".  `setalign(a, offs)` (`compiler_src.cpp:877`) then requires `a`
to be a power of two in `1..65536` and `offs` to be less than `a`, and marks
every start address that is not congruent to `offs` unusable.

Since G2 nothing here is refused.  `align N + M` lowers to `.align N` followed
by `.res M`, the section's label follows the pair so that it lands in the
congruence class the source asked for, and a leading `address` becomes the
same synthetic segment a data block's `address` entry does - which is the
`K816-W0116` below, carrying the `SEGMENTS` line to paste.

The two `K816-E0104` are not a compatibility gap.  `aligned` and `addressed`
carry no width annotation and are reached only by a call, so K816 cannot size
`a = 1`; the original had no width model to be unable to answer.  They are why
this fixture still compares diagnostics rather than bytes - `tests/compat/k816/
align-offset/` is where the emitted pair and the label's position are asserted
against bytes, on a source that can carry `@a8`.

What G1 deliberately did *not* do is let `align 16 + 4` compile.  Before that
task K816 folded the sum and emitted `.align $14`, which places the section at
a multiple of twenty - a different address from either reading of the source,
and emitted without a word (`postmortem.md` §G.1.9b).

K816 differs from the original in one way that is the backend's: `.res M`
emits M filler bytes where the original leaves its linker a gap.  That is the
`backend-different` half of the row.
