**A store into `label + offset` is just a store, and the original says nothing
about it.**  The golden's `main` is

    A9 01        lda #1
    8D 08 20     sta .LT+1        <- into its own instruction stream
    8D 05 20     sta payload+2
    85 81        sta slot+1

which is decision 17 measured: no diagnostic, no comment in the listing, and
the local label `.LT` mangled to `__local1_LT`.

K816 compiles this fixture cleanly today and emits the same three stores in the
same order.  One difference, recorded rather than fixed here: K816 assembles
`slot+1` as `a:slot + $01` where the original used zero page (`85 81`), even
though it does choose direct page for `slot` itself.  The offset form loses
the direct-page decision.  That is G2's to settle - decision 17 says "plain
store, no diagnostic" and says nothing about the addressing mode - so the
manifest row stays `open` until then.

The other difference is layout, and it is not a difference in this compiler:
the original's Atari XL linker prepends a `__start` section at `$2000` holding
`JMP main`, so every address in its golden is three higher than K816's.  A
startup stub is the build's, not the compiler's (`src/abi.rs`), so that part of
the row is `backend-different` by construction.
