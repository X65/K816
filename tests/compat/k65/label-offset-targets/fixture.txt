**A store into `label + offset` is just a store, and the original says nothing
about it.**  The golden's `main` is

    A9 01        lda #1
    8D 08 20     sta .LT+1        <- into its own instruction stream
    8D 05 20     sta payload+2
    85 81        sta slot+1

which is decision 17 measured: no diagnostic, no comment in the listing, and
the local label `.LT` mangled to `__local1_LT`.

K816 compiles this fixture cleanly and emits the same three stores, in the same
order and in the same encodings.

The third one is what G2 changed.  K816 used to assemble `slot+1` as
`a:slot + $01` where the original used zero page (`85 81`), even though it did
choose direct page for `slot` itself: the offset form lost the direct-page
decision.  Decision 17 says "plain store, no diagnostic" and says nothing about
the addressing mode, so G0 left the row `open`; the pre-G2 interview settled it
as **an offset expression inherits the base symbol's address size**, in reads as
well as stores.  `a = slot+1` and `slot+1 = a` disagreeing about the encoding of
the same expression is not something the manifest could have defended.
`resolve.rs::offset_base_name` is the rule, and it is deliberately *not* the
`base_name` the zero-store shortcut asks with - `mem+1 = 0` is still arithmetic
rather than a named place.

The other difference is layout, and it is not a difference in this compiler:
the original's Atari XL linker prepends a `__start` section at `$2000` holding
`JMP main`, so every address in its golden is three higher than K816's.  A
startup stub is the build's, not the compiler's (`src/abi.rs`), so that part of
the row is `backend-different` by construction.
