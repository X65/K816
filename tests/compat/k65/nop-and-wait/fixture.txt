**No `K816-E0001` here any more, and that is the point of the four new
lines.**  Until the manifest audit this fixture expected one, the prose
attributed it to `* N`, and it was actually on `z??` - a *valid K65 spelling*
reaching a generic parse error, which is exactly what decision 0 forbids and
what the `instr-stack-flags` row read `compatible` over.

**Every flag letter's `??`/`!!` is plain `PLP`/`PHP` in the original**: the
letter is decoration, all fourteen spellings mean the same two instructions
(`compiler.inc:167-180`), and this fixture's own golden shows `28 08`.  K816
refuses five of the seven by name - `z c n v i` - because `p??`/`p!!` says
that instruction without pretending to move one flag, and the help names it.

`b` and `d` are the other two and they are *not* refused: they name the
65816's data-bank and direct-page registers, so `b!!` is `PHB` here and `PHP`
there.  That is a K65 spelling with a K816 meaning, which the manifest records
as `instr-stack-flags`'s `intentionally-changed` half rather than leaving it
in the bytes to be found.

`o+` stays out of this source: the 65816 has no `SEV`, the original assembles
`SEv` and dies without a diagnostic, and `instr-flag-set` records that.

**`* N` waits N *cycles*, and it spends an odd one on an illegal opcode.**  The
golden is

    EA           *          one NOP, two cycles
    04 80 EA     * 5        NOP $80 (three cycles) then NOP (two)
    EA EA EA     * 6        three NOPs
    04 80        %          NOP $80 - three cycles, not BRK
    B8           o-         CLV
    68 48 28 08  a?? a!! z?? z!!

`opnop` (`compiler_src.cpp:540`) refuses a negative wait, refuses `* 1` as
unrealizable, refuses more than 256, and spends an odd cycle through
`NOP $80` - the 6502's undocumented three-cycle `NOP zp`, **opcode `$04`**.

That last byte is the whole argument for decision 11.  On the 65816 `$04` is
`TSB dp`, a real instruction that reads and writes memory, so the original's
odd-cycle wait is not merely different on this target - it cannot be
reproduced.  Compatibility here is not cheap, it is impossible, so K816 keeps
its own meaning for `* N` and `%` (`doc/syntax-reference.md`: N NOPs, and
`BRK`), `--strict-k65` warns, and the row is `intentionally-changed` with this
golden as its evidence.

Since G1 there is one `K816-E0001` here, not two: the grammar takes `o` as a
flag name, so `o-` parses.  The one left is `z?? z!!`, which is the status
register spelled `z` - a K65 form no decision has reached yet.

`o-` is the other half of the fixture and needs no argument: `Flag -> o` is the
original's spelling of the overflow flag (`compiler.inc:259-260`) and `B8` is
`CLV`, so decision 14's alias is `compatible`.  Its opposite is not: `o+`
assembles to `SEv`, which does not exist, and the original compiler dies
without a diagnostic.  That is a `legacy-defective` row, and K816 refusing
`o+` is the right behaviour rather than a compatibility break.
