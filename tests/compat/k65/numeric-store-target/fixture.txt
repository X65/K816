**A number cannot be a store target, and the original does not say so - it
aborts.**

`Arg -> Number` is `arg(AM_IMM, $1)` (`compiler.inc:269`): a bare number in
operand position is an *immediate*, not an address.  So `0x10 = 0` reaches
`opmovseq` with an immediate on the left, and the compiler dies inside its own
error path - `oracle.err` records exit 134 with **no diagnostic at all**, the
heap corruption its C++ unwind leaves behind.

That settles what decision 25 left as an assumption.  K816 refuses the same
shape with `K816-E0127`, on the documented rule that a zero-store target must
be a name, and the refusal is not a narrowing of anything: the original has no
program here to be compatible with.  The row stays `K816-extension` because
the construct being classified is the *whole* zero-store family - `NAME = 0`
lowering to `STZ` - and the 6502 the original targets has no `STZ` at all.

`var slot = 0x10` is in the source and unused on purpose: it is the name the
help asks for, sitting at the address the refused line wrote as a number, so
the fixture also shows that the refusal is about the spelling and not about
the address.
