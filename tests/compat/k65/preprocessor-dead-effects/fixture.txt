config    atarixl.cfg

units     input.k65
expect    input.k65 K816-E0101

**A region that is not taken leaves nothing behind — not an assignment, not
a message — and K816 agrees.**

`oracle.err` is one line: `Undeclared eval variable 'SIDE'` at the `[ OUT =
SIDE ]` *below* the region. Two facts fall out of that one line. The bracket
inside `#if 0` did not run, or `SIDE` would have a value; and the `#error`
inside it did not fire, or "boom" would be the diagnostic instead. Both rules
come from the `ST` guard that opens every ordinary semantic action in
`compiler.inc` — the guard `PPIf` and `PPIfNumber` conspicuously lack, which
is what `preprocessor-dead-conditions` beside this one measures.

K816 reports the same thing at the same place, in its own code:
`K816-E0101`, the code an evaluator bracket already uses for a name nothing
gives a value. This is the fixture that keeps the two compilers together on
what a dead region *is*; `preprocessor-dead-conditions` is where they part
company on what a dead region's own directives do.
