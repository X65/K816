**A `;` is punctuation and moves no byte, at the top level and after a
statement alike.**  The golden is `27 AD 03 20 60` for a program with six of
them in it, and K816's is the same five bytes.

Two productions, neither with a semantic action: `TopElem -> ;`
(`compiler.inc:29`) and `Instr -> Instr ;` (`:149`).  K65 is
newline-insensitive, so the `;` is a thing an author may write rather than a
separator the language needs - which is why it can also be absent everywhere
else in this corpus.

This fixture answers to two rows, `topelem-empty` and `instr-semicolon`, and
the manifest cites it from both.  Each read "K816 already accepts one" from G0
until G10a with nothing to say so; the `Fixture` column read `—` and
`tests/compat.rs` skipped them.  That is the shape the fixture-witness gate was
added for, one step earlier: a row nothing checks.

One difference from the oracle image and it is the linker's - the `4C 04 20`
`JMP main` stub K816 does not emit.
