**`align N + M` lowers to `.align N` then `.res M`, and the section's label
follows the pair.**

Decision 15 says what `align N + M` means and `k65/align-and-address` is the
original's answer proving it; this is K816's side of the same claim, asserted
at the bytes.  It has to be a separate fixture because the oracle source
carries no width annotations - the original had no width model - so K816
cannot size its immediates and it can only compare diagnostics.

Both readings the lowering has to get right are here:

  - `table` is at `$2004`, not at a multiple of twenty.  Folding `16 + 4` into
    `align 20` is what K816 did before sub-plan G, silently
    (`postmortem.md` §G.1.9b).
  - the label comes *after* `.res`, so it names the congruent address rather
    than the boundary.  Emitting it first would put the section's name four
    bytes before its bytes.

K816 differs from the original in one way, and it is the backend's: `.res M`
emits M filler bytes where the original left its linker a gap.  ld65 fills them
with `$00` here because `atarixl.cfg` says `fillval = $00`.

One thing this fixture found: `.align` inside a segment makes ld65 warn unless
the segment itself declares at least that alignment, even when its start
address already satisfies it.  `atarixl.cfg` says `align = $100` on `CODE` for
that reason, and `doc/syntax-reference.md` says so where `align` is described.
