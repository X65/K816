**Three spellings, one code, and the wording is a removal.**  Two top-level
forms — by identifier and by string — and the block form.  All of them keep
`K816-K65-0001`, because they are one construct seen from three sides.

Three reports, not six, and the reason is in the grammar rather than in this
task: an `image` entry's arguments are a *loose run* that ends where the next
data entry begins, and `tiles`, `colormode` and `imgwave` are identifiers, so
they are swallowed as arguments of the `image` above them.  Refusing the
whole run once is the right shape — the modifiers have no meaning without the
reader — and it is what `data-image-tiles` records.

Until G6 the message read "K65 `image` construct is not implemented yet",
which promised something.  Nothing brings an image reader back: reading a
bitmap and scanning it into tiles is an asset pipeline, and `doc/design.md` §1
says K816 does not own one.  So the title says "is not a K816 construct", the
note says why, and the help names what to do instead — convert the image
outside the build and include the bytes with `binary`.  No adapter tool ships
with the compiler.

`binary` is the replacement the help names, and it works: `k65/binary-inclusion`
is the same shape with the bitmap already converted.
