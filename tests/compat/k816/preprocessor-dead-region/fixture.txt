**A condition is evaluated only when it can decide something.**

Three undeclared names and one refusing bracket sit in this file, and none of
them is reported, because none of them is in a region that could be compiled:
two are nested inside `#if 0`, and one is the condition of an `#elif` whose
chain already took its branch. The single byte `$5A` is the whole assertion —
it is `MARK` from the branch that *was* taken, so the file compiled, and it
compiled without a word about the four things inside the regions that were
not.

The original disagrees on both counts, and the disagreement is measured:
`k65/preprocessor-dead-conditions` is its refusal on the nested condition, and
`k65/preprocessor-condition-reads` shows a top-level `#elif` chain deciding
correctly while still evaluating what it does not need. `PPIf` and
`PPIfNumber` are the only rules in `compiler.inc` with no `ST` guard, so a
condition there runs unconditionally.

K816 takes C's rule instead. The cost is that a typo inside `#if 0` waits
until someone turns the region on; the gain is that `#if 0` is usable for
what everybody uses it for, which is the reason the divergence was chosen
rather than inherited.
