**A directive in an `inline` body is decided once, at the definition.**

Three expansions, one `K816-W0151`. That is decision 9's rule, and with the
preprocessor as a pass that runs before analysis it is not a rule anyone has
to enforce — expansion happens in lowering, long after the region is gone.
The diagnostic count is the assertion: a preprocessor that ran per expansion
would say it three times.

The bytes say the other half. `y++` is the branch `#if FAST` took, so the
three expansions are three `INY`, and nothing anywhere is an `INX`. The
original cannot be asked: `#warn` below the top level aborts it
(`k816/preprocessor-messages`), and `inline` bodies are `Sequence`, which is
one of the two levels its `#elif` handling also aborts on.
