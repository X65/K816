**Every way a region can fail to pair, reported rather than parsed around.**

The original has no diagnostic for any of these. Its regions are a *grammar*
production — `TopElem -> PPIf TopList TopListPPElse PPEndif` — so three of
the four shapes are a bare "syntax error" with a line number and nothing
else, and the fourth, a region crossing a `}`, aborts the compiler outright
(`free(): invalid pointer`, exit 134). K816 reports each one with both spans:
the directive that cannot pair, and the directive or brace it should have
paired with.

Six diagnostics for four shapes, because two of them are one mistake seen
from two sides:

  - the leading `#endif`, and the `#else` after it, each with no `#if` above;
  - the second `#else` of one chain, and the `#elif` that follows it;
  - the `#if` inside `main`, reported at the `}` that ended its level;
  - and that region's `#endif`, which arrives at the top level where no
    frame is open.

The last pair is what "a region is structural" means in practice: liveness is
decided per element list, and an element list stops at its closing brace.
Reporting both sides is deliberate — the author moved one of the two, and
only they know which.
