A zero store becomes STZ only where the accumulator's value is not wanted
afterwards.

The legacy compiler kept `A9 00 85 12` before the `tax`, reasoning that A is
live.  K816 emits `A9 00 64 12 AA`: it keeps the `lda #0` the transfer needs
and still uses STZ for the store, because STZ does not disturb A.  Same length,
same architectural state, a different route - `backend-different`.
