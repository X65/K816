`image` with `tiles` and `colormode` inside a data block, and `binary` beside

`image` is refused (`K816-K65-0001`, twice) and `binary` compiles: since G6 it
is an `.incbin`, and `table.bin` sits beside this source because that is where
K816 looks - the directory of the file that wrote the line.

The two refusals are the two `image` entries.  `tiles`, `colormode` and
`imgwave` report *with* the `image` above them rather than separately: an
entry's arguments are a loose run that ends where the next data entry begins,
and all three are identifiers, so they are swallowed as arguments.  That is
the right shape - none of them means anything without the reader - and it is
what `data-image-tiles` records.
