A `for ... eval` range whose body reads a name a top-level `[ FACTOR = 3 ]`
block assigned.

Since G4 it compiles, and the claim is the bytes rather than the absence of
diagnostics: `ramp_up` is `00 03 06 09 0C` and `ramp_down` is `04 03 02 01 00`.
Between them they pin four things - that a top-level block runs, that the name
it assigns reaches a bracket further down the file, that an inclusive range is
five values and not four, and that a reversed range counts down.

Before G4 the block was `K816-E0201` and `FACTOR` was `K816-E0101`.
