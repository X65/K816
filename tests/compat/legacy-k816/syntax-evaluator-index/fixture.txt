`index` and the `[ ]` indexing operator.

The oracle answer that matters is in `tests/compat/k65/eval-forms/`: what is
indexed is a **section** - a data label - not an array value, and `tab[i]` is
exactly `index(tab, i)`.  Reading a section's bytes means having assembled the
section, which K816 does not do until lowering, so G4 names the form and G6
implements it: one `K816-E0201`, at `arr[1]`.

The two `K816-E0101` are `B` and `C` in `main`, and they show how far the
refusal reaches.  `A` is assigned before the deferral and survives - `a=[A]`
compiles - while `B` and `C` are the two the aborted block never got to
assign.  An evaluation stops at its first unanswerable question and keeps
everything it had already decided, which is the same shape the original has
and the reason `A` is in this fixture at all.

Before G4: four `K816-E0201`, one per bracket.
