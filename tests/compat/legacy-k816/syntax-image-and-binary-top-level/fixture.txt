`image NAME = "file"` and `binary NAME = "file"` at top level.

Two readings collide here and the original wins (decision 2).  The **legacy
K816** compiled these into labels with no bytes behind them - its
`expected.lst` is three `lda $00`.  The **original** builds a whole section
from the file's bytes for `binary`, and then cannot resolve the label, which
`k65/binary-top-level` measures.

Since G6, `Blob` resolves: K816 compiles the declaration as the section it
describes, `payload.bin` sits beside this source, and the load reads real
bytes.  The two `K816-E0101` that remain are `SpriteSheet` and `RawSprite` -
the names the two `image` declarations would have declared, and `image` is
removed (decision 7).  Refusing a declaration means the name it would have
introduced is not there, and a source porting off `image` has to convert the
bitmap and name the result, which is what the help says.
