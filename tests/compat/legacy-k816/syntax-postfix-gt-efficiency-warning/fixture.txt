`} >` is the other K816 extension postfix, and here the legacy wording is
wrong for K816.

The legacy compiler warned that `} >` "expands to three branch instructions"
and named the expansion `BEQ skip` + `BCC skip` + `BRA target`.  K816 emits
`E8 F0 02 B0 FB 60`: `inx`, `beq` past the loop, `bcs` back to it - **two**
branches.  G8's warning has to say what K816 does, not what the legacy
compiler did (decision 23 says "with the legacy compiler's wording", and this
fixture is why that has to be read as "with the reason the legacy wording
gave": the expansion is the reason, and K816's expansion is shorter).
