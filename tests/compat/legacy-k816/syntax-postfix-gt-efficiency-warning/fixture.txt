`} >` is the other K816 extension postfix, and here the legacy wording is
wrong for K816.

The legacy compiler warned that `} >` "expands to three branch instructions"
and named the expansion `BEQ skip` + `BCC skip` + `BRA target`.  K816 emits
`E8 F0 02 B0 FB 60`: `inx`, `beq` past the loop, `bcs` back to it - **two**
branches.  G8's warning says what K816 does, not what the legacy compiler did
(decision 23 says "with the legacy compiler's wording", and this fixture is
why that has to be read as "with the reason the legacy wording gave": the
expansion is the reason, and K816's expansion is shorter).

`K816-W0157` is that warning.  It is decided at lowering, where the emitted
pair is known, rather than from the written token - which is what lets the
note name `beq` and `bcs` here and the other pair in the `} <=` fixture
beside this one.
