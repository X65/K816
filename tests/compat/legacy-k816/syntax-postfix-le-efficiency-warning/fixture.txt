`} <=` is a K816 extension - the original's `BranchOp` has no `<=`
(`compiler.inc:304-319`) - and the legacy compiler warned that it expands to
two branches.

K816 emits `E8 90 FD F0 FB 60`: `inx`, `bcc` back to the loop, `beq` back to
the loop.  Two branches, the same expansion the legacy warning described.
G8 added the warning: `K816-W0157`, whose note names this pair.
