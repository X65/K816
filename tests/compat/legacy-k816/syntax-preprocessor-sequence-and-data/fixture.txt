Preprocessor regions at statement and data-entry level, with `#if !0` among
the conditions.

Compiles since G5: `a=1` from the statement-level region, `3 4` from the
data-entry one.

The original *aborts* on this file — `#elif` at any level but the top is a
`free(): invalid pointer` and a SIGABRT there, because its grammar gives
`PPElIf` a production under `TopList` and none under `Sequence` or `DataList`
even though `#if`/`#else`/`#endif` have all three (`compiler.inc:98-101`,
`:142-145`).  K816 supports the directive uniformly at all three levels,
which is a strict superset and costs nothing; `k816/preprocessor-levels`
covers it and the `ppif-directives` row records the divergence.

Seven `K816-E0201` stood here before G5, one per directive.  Before G1 there
were eight: the extra one was a `K816-E0001` on `!0`, because B's grammar made
the condition a K65 `_expression` and K65 has no prefix `!`.  G1 makes the
condition an *evaluator* expression, which is where `!` lives.
